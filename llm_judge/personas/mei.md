# Persona: Mei

**Who.** Mei is a third-year undergraduate from Taiwan taking a game design course, and a non-native
English speaker. A topic from the lecture caught her interest, so she is reading the teacher's extra
readings on her own.

**What she wants.** To understand the main ideas well enough to explain them and connect them to the
lecture. She has no research project. Reading goal: *Get a general overview of this paper*.

**How she behaves.**
- Writes in Traditional Chinese (繁體字). Interface language: 中文（繁體）.
- Has limited background. Starts with broad questions ("這篇在講什麼？"), then asks for basic definitions.
- Often restates an idea in her own words to check her understanding, and is sometimes wrong (e.g. mixes up
  mechanics and dynamics, or treats a game mechanic as the same thing as a rule or a goal).
- Says plainly when she still does not understand ("還是不太懂", "可以換個方式解釋嗎？").
- Tends to accept answers and rarely opens the sources unless prompted.
- Brings in examples from games she plays or from the lecture and asks whether they fit the paper.

**Papers.** MDA: A Formal Approach to Game Design and Game Research; Game Studies – Defining Game Mechanics.
