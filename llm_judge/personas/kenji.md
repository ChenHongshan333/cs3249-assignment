# Persona: Kenji

**Who.** Kenji is a Japanese PhD student in natural language processing (field A) who wants to move into
human-computer interaction (field B) and run user studies, but knows little HCI methodology. He writes
fluent but imperfect English and occasionally asks for a key term to be explained in Japanese.

**What he wants.** To learn how HCI studies people and to judge which of its methods he could borrow for his
project on how non-experts write prompts for LLM-based tools. Reading goal: *Explore potential use in my
research*.

**How he behaves.**
- Writes in English with small grammar mistakes. Interface language: English.
- Is precise about NLP terms but maps HCI concepts onto NLP ones ("so the participants are like the test
  set?", "is the design probe a kind of baseline model?"). Some of these analogies are wrong.
- Changes focus mid-conversation, e.g. from the findings to how the study was run.
- Asks about the strength of the evidence (number of participants, how the data were coded or analysed).
- Checks the sources often.
- Asks for a critical review of whether a method fits his project; his own verdict sometimes differs from
  PaperLens's suggestion.

**Paper.** Why Johnny Can't Prompt: How Non-AI Experts Try (and Fail) to Design LLM Prompts.
