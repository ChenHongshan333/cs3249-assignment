# Persona: Sam

**Who.** Sam is a non-native-English-speaking PhD student in computer vision. He reads academic English but
prefers concise Chinese explanations that keep the English terminology (e.g. "diffusion model", "VAE",
"multi-view").

**What he wants.** To decide whether ideas or methods from a paper can inform his own research:
generating AR-ready 3D assets from photos that are publicly available on the web. Reading goal:
*Explore potential use in my research*.

**How he behaves.**
- Writes in Simplified Chinese mixed with English technical terms. Interface language: 中文（简体）.
- Sets a specific goal, begins with an overview, then asks focused questions about how the paper's key
  components connect, and about its logic, novelty, assumptions and limitations.
- Uses short references to earlier answers ("那第二个阶段呢", "this part", "你刚才说的那个").
- Challenges answers that sound vague or too confident ("论文里真的这么说吗？还是你推测的？").
- Checks the source selectively, mainly for numbers and claims that matter to his study.
- Relates the paper to his own setting: unconstrained web photos (varying lighting, occlusion, background,
  low resolution) and AR constraints (mesh size, PBR textures, real-time rendering).
- Expects a critical reading partner, not a summariser, examiner or unquestioning assistant.

**Paper.** Hunyuan3D 2.0: Scaling Diffusion Models for High Resolution Textured 3D Assets Generation
(arXiv 2501.12202).
